    HOW TO PLAY

    A game is a few rounds. In every round, everyone sings once.

    Lucky DrawOne secret card each
    Everyone's turnOne by one, each player sings
    Round doneStandings, then the next round

    One turn Every player's turn on the mic goes like this

    1. Song pickThe crowd votes between three songs. The singer gets the winner.
    2. SingThe crowd cheers, plays cards and chases secret missions.
    3. VoteEveryone rates the song from 1 to 5, in secret.
    4. VerdictThe score goes up and the points land.

    Room rules The host sets them in the lobby

    RoundsHow many times everyone sings (3 to start).
    LengthHow long each song runs. Short ones start at the chorus.
    Crowd voicesHear the crowd while someone sings: off, far away or live.
    CardsOff, Classic (three sabotages each) or Lucky (a new card every round).
    TomatoesLet the crowd throw tomatoes, or keep it friendly.
    MissionsA secret mission for everyone in the crowd, every song.

    In Lucky mode every round starts with a draw. Your card is secret: the others only see how many you hold. Use it this round or lose it.

    Only the votes decide the score. Cheers, laughs and tomatoes just move the hype meter.
